#  Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior using transaction data to uncover purchasing trends, customer segments, revenue drivers, and subscription patterns. By combining Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, and Power BI, the project transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support customer retention, marketing optimization, and revenue growth strategies.

The analysis covers over **3,900 customer transactions**, providing a comprehensive view of customer engagement, spending behavior, and product performance.

---

##  Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and shopping trends.
- Identify repeat buyers and customer retention patterns.
- Evaluate subscription adoption and growth opportunities.
- Assess the impact of discounts on purchasing decisions.
- Build business intelligence dashboards for data-driven decision-making.
- Generate recommendations to improve revenue and customer loyalty.
